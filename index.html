The quiet cost of keeping the lights on and the oven running — electricity, insurance, software, rent. It's real, even though no single ingredient represents it.

What are Transaction Fees? ▸

The slice of each sale that never makes it to your pocket — card processing, marketplace commissions, delivery. They scale with your price, so they change how high a price needs to be to hit your target margin.

Profitability Dashboard
Revenue vs. Profit per hour ▸

Revenue per labor hour is everything customers pay, divided by your hours. Business profit per labor hour is what's actually left in your pocket after every cost — including the wage you already paid yourself — divided by hours. Revenue per hour will always look bigger; profit per hour is the number that matters.
