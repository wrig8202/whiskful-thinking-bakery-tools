🎯 Target Profit Margin
%
Margin = what percentage of your selling price is profit.
Formula: Price = Cost ÷ (1 − Margin%)
At 30% margin on a $1.00 cost: $1.00 ÷ 0.70 = $1.43 → profit $0.43 = 30% of $1.43 ✓

Markup = how much you added on top of cost.
Formula: Price = Cost × (1 + Markup%)
At 30% markup on a $1.00 cost: $1.00 × 1.30 = $1.30 → but the margin is only 23%, not 30%.

This calculator always uses the correct margin formula so your profit target is actually hit.
